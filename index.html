The story behind the paint

Hi, I'm Vic!

Vibrant Visions by Vic started after I attended a paint-and-sip event at my workplace, The GOAT Sports Pub & Pizzeria. I got to see the behind-the-scenes details and learn what guests loved — and what could make the experience even better.

I wanted to create a welcoming space where our restaurant regulars could feel right at home, while new guests could enjoy a host who knows both the paint party and the restaurant. I've always had a passion for painting. I'm not claiming to be a professional artist — I simply love creating, having fun, and watching everyone discover what they can make.

My goal is simple: bring people together, make everyone feel welcome, and leave with a painting and a memory to take home.

Painting with love, Vic

Private Paint Parties

Private Events

$100 deposit

A non-refundable $100 deposit is required to begin creating personalized painting ideas for your event.

  • No minimum number of painters
  • At least $200 in event spending after the deposit
  • At least one month’s notice is required for preparations
  • Custom painting ideas for your group
  • I can travel to your location
